Community and Communication
===========================

Channels
- Public discussion: [ROS Discourse](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/c/community-groups/openembedded/26)
- Real-time chat: [OpenRobotics Zulip Chat](https://openrobotics.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/526059-OpenEmbedded)
- Issues & PRs: [GitHub meta-ros](https://github.com/ros/meta-ros)
- Meetings: [Biweekly community meeting (Google Meet)](https://meet.google.com/bxp-triq-yvr)

Onboarding
- New contributors: follow `templates/CONTRIBUTING.md` and the Ubuntu→Yocto migration quickstart (spec under `specs/`).

Events and Growth
- Monthly office hours, mentorship pairing, contribution sprints.
- Celebrate first-time contributors and provide clear low-effort issues for onboarding.

Code of Conduct
- Follow project norms; maintain a respectful, inclusive community.
